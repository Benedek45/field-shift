## Findings
- Cowpea, sunn hemp and lablab produced 1.2 to 3.0 tonnes per ha of biomass in an 8-9 week summer fallow in semi-arid southern Australia, while balansa clover and barrel medic produced less than 1.0 tonne per ha.
- The share of legume nitrogen derived from the atmosphere ranged from 39% in barrel medic to 73% in sunn hemp in the field.
- Biological nitrogen fixation by cowpea and sunn hemp in the field was 46 to 55 kg N per ha, against about 26 kg N per ha for lablab and mung bean.
- The authors describe about 50 kg N per ha for cowpea and sunn hemp with supplementary irrigation as the likely upper limit of nitrogen contribution in hot, dry summer conditions.
- Any increase in summer cover crop biomass has implications for the water balance and for the following cash crop.
## Conditions and limits
- Semi-arid Mediterranean-type climate with a short, hot summer fallow; the nitrogen figures do not transfer directly to a humid Corn Belt winter cover crop.
- The glasshouse experiment gave much lower nitrogen fixation shares (15% to 33%) because soil mineral nitrogen was higher there.
- Small-plot field trial with supplementary irrigation; without irrigation the biomass and nitrogen contribution would be lower.
