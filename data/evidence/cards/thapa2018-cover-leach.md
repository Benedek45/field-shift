## Findings
- Meta-analysis of 238 observations from 28 studies: non-leguminous cover crops reduced nitrate leaching by 56% on average.
- Non-legume plus legume cover crop mixtures reduced nitrate leaching as effectively as non-legumes alone, and significantly more than legumes alone.
- Nitrate leaching reductions were larger on coarse-textured soils and in drier years, and were enhanced by earlier planting dates and greater cover crop shoot biomass.
## Conditions and limits
- Only 28 studies met the inclusion criteria, and the authors note that missing variance information in most published work limits how precisely the effect can be characterised.
- The 56% average is for non-leguminous cover crops; a pure legume cover can leach as much nitrate as no cover because of its own nitrogen release.
- Effects depend on establishment: late planting and thin stands reduce the reduction in leaching.
