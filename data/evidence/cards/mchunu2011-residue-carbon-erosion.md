## Findings
- With crop residue cover below 10% during the rainy season, no-till raised topsoil soil organic carbon stocks by 26% compared with conventional tillage.
- No-till reduced soil loss by 68%, from 301.5 to 96.8 g per m2 per year, on 22.5 m2 runoff plots.
- The study was set up because residue is too valuable to smallholders to be left on the surface, which limits the soil-protection benefit of no-till.
## Conditions and limits
- South African smallholder maize system, not a US row-crop farm: use the mechanism (residue cover protects soil, no-till alone does not) rather than the absolute erosion rates.
- Plots of 22.5 m2 are small, and the comparison is no-till against conventional tillage on the same soil since 2002.
- The abstract does not report runoff volume separately from soil loss in the passages read here.
