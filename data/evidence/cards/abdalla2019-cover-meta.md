## Findings
- The review covered 106 studies at 372 sites; cover crops significantly decreased nitrogen leaching and significantly increased soil organic carbon sequestration, with no significant effect on direct nitrous oxide emissions.
- Across the studies, cover crops mitigated the net greenhouse gas balance by 2.06 plus or minus 2.10 Mg CO2-eq per hectare per year.
- The primary crop grain yield was about 4% lower with a cover crop than in the control treatment.
- Mixed cover crops containing both legumes and non-legumes increased primary crop yield by about 13%, so the yield penalty is not inherent to cover cropping.
## Conditions and limits
- Most of the data come from studies lasting 2-3 years, so long-term effects are weakly represented.
- Results are pooled across climates, soils and cover crop species; the authors state that management must be adapted to specific soil, management and regional climatic conditions.
- Nitrous oxide and albedo effects are measured only in part of the literature, so the greenhouse gas balance figure should not be read as a full life-cycle number.
