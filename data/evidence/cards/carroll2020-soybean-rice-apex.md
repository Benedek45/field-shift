## Findings
- The APEX model was calibrated on five Arkansas fields under soybean-rice rotation from 2017 to 2019 and reproduced leaf area index with R2 above 0.66 and percent bias within 32%.
- Modelled against observed yield, percent bias and percent difference were within 2.5% for rice and 15% for soybean.
- Over 20-year simulations, rice-rice and soybean-rice rotation yields were not statistically different, contrary to the expectation that rotation raises yield.
- Reducing irrigation by more than 20% below the farmers' original amounts significantly reduced yields in all fields except one that had been over-irrigated.
## Conditions and limits
- Model output, not measured 20-year results: the rotation and irrigation scenarios inherit the calibration error, and soybean is less well reproduced than rice (15% versus 2.5%).
- Five fields in one Arkansas region, with irrigation practices typical of Delta rice (cascade flooding, multiple-inlet polypipe, furrow).
- The null rotation result is for rice and soybean only; it does not test rotations that include a third crop.
