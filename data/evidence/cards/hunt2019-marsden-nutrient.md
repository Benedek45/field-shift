## Findings
- Modelled with SWAT on the Marsden Farm plots, total nitrogen runoff loss was up to 39% lower and total phosphorus runoff loss up to 30% lower in the more diverse 3-year and 4-year rotations than in the 2-year corn-soybean system.
- Nitrate leaching losses were not affected by cropping system, so the water-quality gain was in surface runoff rather than in leaching.
- Diversification reduced erosion losses by up to 60%.
- The 3-year and 4-year systems maintained or increased crop yields and net returns relative to the 2-year conventional system.
- Reducing herbicide use intensity did not change nutrient and sediment losses, crop yields or profitability.
## Conditions and limits
- Runoff and erosion numbers come from the ArcSWAT model calibrated with plot measurements, not from measured edge-of-field loads at field scale.
- One Iowa site on one soil; the ranking of rotations can differ where leaching rather than runoff dominates the loss pathway.
- The comparison keeps the same crops in all systems (corn, soybean, small grain, forage legume), so it does not test adding a new crop species to the region.
