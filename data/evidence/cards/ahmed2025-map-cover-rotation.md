## Findings
- Voluntary winter cover crop adoption in the Mississippi Alluvial Plain increased by 5.3% of the total between 2013 and 2019.
- Four predominant cash crop rotations were associated with cover crop use, and a soybean-soybean rotation had the greatest association with increased winter cover crop acreage.
- Cover crop locations were identified from remote sensing, ground-truthed government data and the USDA Cropland Data Layer rather than from farm records.
## Conditions and limits
- Satellite and CDL-based classification: species, seeding date and termination date are not observed, and classification error is not zero.
- The association between soybean-soybean and cover crops is a correlation in adoption patterns, not evidence that continuous soybean is agronomically sound.
- Regional aggregate: field-level decisions and the reasons farmers adopted cover crops are outside what the data can show.
